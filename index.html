Operating Systems: What Runs Your Devices?

Windows, macOS, Android and iOS: let’s explore the software behind every screen.

Question for the class:

Which phone system do you use: Android or iOS? Why?

Start like this: “I use … because …”

Lesson goals. By the end you can:
  • name five functions of an operating system;
  • compare four systems using five criteria;
  • use ten key words in sentences.

Plan: warm-up 5 min · theory 15 · group work 15 · presentations 10 · wrap-up 5
